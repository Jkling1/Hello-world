# Lane 3: Training & Performance — Project IronMind 12-Month Transformation Protocol

## Outcome
- Deliver a systems-first 12-month roadmap that converts a 300-lb athlete into a durable Ironman finisher-in-progress with daily, weekly, and monthly execution cues.

## Assumptions
- **ATHLETE_PROFILE:** 34-year-old, 300 lbs, cleared for progressive training, past knee discomfort managed with strength work.
- **PRIMARY_TARGET:** Full Ironman (Nov Year 2) with interim Ironman 70.3 at Month 9; current focus is Year 1 transformation.
- **SCHEDULE_CONSTRAINTS:** 10–12 training hours/week average, long run Sunday AM, masters swim Tue/Thu, gym access M/W/F, remote work flexibility.
- **GEAR_STACK:** Carbon-plated trainers, endurance bike with power meter, Garmin watch, basic wetsuit.
- **CONTENT_CHANNELS:** Strava + Instagram updates twice weekly.
- **OFFER_TIER:** Internal protocol; athlete self-executing with remote coach touchpoints.
- **VOICE_NOTES:** Avoid banned words; lean into “relentless, precise, accountable.”

## Protocol / Steps
- **Phase 1 – Architecture (Month 1)**
  - Week 1 diagnostics: DEXA, lactate/threshold testing, bike fit, gait analysis, mobility FMS.
  - Build master calendar with anchor events, family obligations, deload weeks every 4th week.
  - Establish daily checklist: Morning HRV log, hydration (30 ml/kg), protein target (1 g/lb lean mass), 10k steps.
  - Launch feedback loop: Monday planning, Thursday mid-week audit, Sunday performance review.
- **Phase 2 – Foundation Building (Months 2-4)**
  - Training minimums: Run 3x (Zone 2, 30–50 min), Bike 2x (trainer intervals, 60–75 min), Swim 2x (technique focus, 45 min), Lift 2x (total-body strength, 45 min).
  - Nutrition: 500 kcal deficit average, 160 g protein/day, 90 g carbs pre long sessions, fueling log nightly.
  - Sleep system: In-bed 10:00 PM, devices off 9:15 PM, Oura/Whoop readiness tracked; adapt training when readiness <60.
  - Mobility: Daily 10-min hip/ankle routine; weekly 60-min physiotherapy or yoga session.
- **Phase 3 – Capacity Expansion (Months 5-8)**
  - Periodization: 3-week build, 1-week deload; volume peaks at 12 hrs.
  - Run: 4x/week (1 long aerobic up to 2 hr, 1 tempo progression, 1 hill repeat, 1 easy). Bike: 3x/week (sweet spot, VO2 micro-intervals, long endurance up to 4 hr). Swim: 3x/week (threshold sets, open-water skills, recovery technique).
  - Strength: Shift to heavy lifts 2x/week + functional core; add plyometrics once readiness stable.
  - Testing: Monthly threshold re-test, quarterly FTP ramp, body comp photo log.
  - Recovery: 1 massage/month, compression boots post long days, rest HR >10% above baseline triggers load reduction.
- **Phase 4 – Peak Preparation (Months 9-11)**
  - Specificity: Back-to-back long ride/run bricks (Sat 4.5h bike + 45-min run, Sun 2.5h run). Race simulations with nutrition dress rehearsals.
  - Intolerance training: Heat sessions (sauna/bike), mental toughness drills (overnight trainer ride, “no-music” long runs).
  - Taper logic: 3-week taper before 70.3 (volume -20/-40/-60%), maintain intensity touches.
  - Equipment: Dial aero position, race-day kit testing, nutrition storage solutions practiced.
- **Phase 5 – Execution & Legacy (Month 12)**
  - Race execution blueprint: Pacing by power/HR, fueling schedule (90 g carbs/hr bike, 70 g run, 800-1000 mg sodium/hr).
  - Post-race debrief within 48 hrs: Metrics review, wins/gaps, system refinements.
  - Identity codification: Write IronMind Charter 2.0, define next-year targets, create storytelling assets for community.

## Risks & Mitigations
- **Injury flare-ups** (knee/soft tissue) → Integrate 2x weekly prehab, monitor acute:chronic workload ratio (<1.3), instant deload upon pain >3/10.
- **Weight-loss plateau** → Monthly nutrition audit; adjust macros; add NEAT (2x15-min walks) and ensure sleep quality >80 score.
- **Burnout/compliance drop** → Enforce deloads, schedule quarterly adventure training (trail camp), leverage accountability partner for Sunday reviews.
- **Logistics conflicts** → Calendar buffer of 15%; designate Friday PM as catch-up session window.
